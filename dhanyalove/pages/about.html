Food shouldn't go to waste while people go hungry

Hunger Hub connects surplus food to the people, animals, and systems that need it — before it becomes waste.

The Problem

Every day, edible food from homes, events, and restaurants is thrown away — while people nearby go without a meal. Most of this happens because there is no fast, safe, reliable way to move surplus food to someone who needs it in time.

  • 1/3 of all food produced globally is wasted
  • Millions go hungry within a few kilometers of that waste
  • No system exists to route it before it spoils
🍲
📦

The Hunger Hub Solution

Donors upload surplus food to their nearest smart collection box. The food is tracked in real time through a defined lifecycle — first offered to people, then, after the human-access window closes, redirected to animal feed, and finally to biogas collection so nothing is wasted.

  1. Food Provider Uploads Food
  2. Nearest Hunger Hub Box
  3. Available for Humans
  4. Animal Feed Section
  5. Biogas Collection

Impact

People Fed

Surplus food reaches people first, while it's still safe and fresh.

Animals Fed

Food past human consumption time still feeds animals instead of landfills.

Zero Waste

What's left becomes biogas — closing the loop completely.
