From surplus to service, tracked every step of the way

One donation moves through four clear stages before anything is wasted.

🧑‍🍳

1. Donor Uploads Food

A food provider logs in, fills in food details — name, category, quantity, veg/non-veg, prep and expiry time — accepts the food safety disclaimer, and submits.

→
📍

2. Routed to Nearest Box

The donation is assigned to the nearest Hunger Hub smart box based on the donor's location and box capacity.

→
🍽️

3. Available for Humans

The food is listed as available for human consumption until its set expiry window closes.

→
🐾

4. Animal Feeding Stage

Once the human-access window expires, remaining food automatically moves to the animal feed section.

→
♻️

5. Biogas Stage

What's left after animal feeding is marked for pickup by a registered biogas collector.

→
✅

6. Collected

The collector confirms pickup, closing the donation's lifecycle in the tracking system.

What is a Smart Box?

A Hunger Hub box is a physical collection point where donors drop off food and collectors pick it up. Each box reports its live location and available capacity, so donors and collectors always know where to go.

  • Live capacity shown on the map
  • Fixed location for easy, repeat drop-offs
  • Timestamped intake for every item
🗄️

Who's Involved

Donor

Uploads food, agrees to the safety disclaimer, and is responsible for its quality.

Recipient

Accesses food directly from the box during the human-consumption window. No volunteers involved.

Collector

Registered biogas collectors pick up remaining waste and earn points per collection.
